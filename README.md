# Leader Week Log

A free, private weekly tracker for leadership development. It was built for participants in the Aspire Leaders Program, but anyone can use it.

## What it does

- **SMART goal:** write your goal in one sentence, fill in each part of the SMART framework and see a countdown to your deadline.
- **Daily habits:** add the habits that support your goal, tick them off each day and track your weekly target and streak.
- **Top 3 priorities:** choose three priorities for each week and mark them done.
- **Sunday review:** note what went well, what you postponed and one change for next week.
- **Progress stats:** habit completion, priorities completed, weeks reviewed and your best streak.

## Privacy

There is no account and no server. Your entries are saved only in your own browser. Use **Copy backup** and **Restore** on the page to move your data to another device.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in any browser, or publish it with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch).
