# Leader Week Log

A free, private weekly tracker for leadership development. Set one SMART goal, build the daily habits that support it, choose your top three priorities each week and review your progress every Sunday.

It was built for participants in the Aspire Leaders Program, but anyone can use it.

**Open the tracker:** https://fizzamubeen.github.io/leader-week-log/

No sign-up, no app to install, and it works on phones and computers.

## What's inside

| Section | What it's for |
|---|---|
| SMART goal | Write your goal in one sentence and fill in Specific, Measurable, Attainable and Relevant. A start date and deadline give you a countdown and a time-elapsed bar. |
| Daily habits | Add small daily actions, set a target number of days per week, and tick them off. You see your weekly progress and your current streak. |
| Top 3 priorities | Choose three priorities for each week and mark them done. |
| Sunday review | Note what went well, what you postponed and one change for next week. |
| Progress stats | Habit completion this week, priorities completed overall, weeks reviewed and your best streak. |

## How to use it

1. **Open the link above.** The tracker starts with example data so you can see how it works.
2. **Press _Start fresh_** in the green bar to clear the examples.
3. **Write your SMART goal.** Use the Aspire format: _"Over the next [timeframe], I will improve my [leadership behaviour] by [specific actions], and I will measure my progress through [indicators]."_ Then set a start date and deadline.
4. **Add 2–4 habits** that move the goal forward, for example "Plan tomorrow's top task" or "30 minutes of study", with a target such as 5 days a week.
5. **Tick a day's box** each time you complete a habit.
6. **Every Monday,** write your top 3 priorities for the week.
7. **Every Sunday,** fill in the review. Use the arrows at the top to move between weeks.
8. **Back up now and then.** Press _Copy backup_ and save the text in a note or email to yourself.

### Moving to another device

Your data is stored only in the browser you use. To continue on another phone or computer:

1. On the old device, press **Copy backup**.
2. Send the copied text to yourself.
3. On the new device, open the tracker, paste the text into the backup box and press **Restore from pasted backup**.

### Add it to your phone's home screen

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → Add to Home Screen.
- **Android (Chrome):** menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen.

## Privacy

There is no account, no server and no tracking. Everything you type stays in your own browser. Nobody else, including the person who shared the link, can see your entries.

Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, will erase your log, so keep a backup.

## Accessibility

- Works with keyboard only, with visible focus outlines.
- Every checkbox and button has a label for screen readers.
- Follows your device's light or dark mode.
- Respects the "reduce motion" setting.

## Run your own copy

The whole app is a single file, `index.html`.

- **Use it offline:** download `index.html` and open it in any browser.
- **Host your own copy:** fork this repository, then go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, and save. Your copy will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/leader-week-log/` within a minute or two.
