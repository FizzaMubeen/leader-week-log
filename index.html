How to use Leader Week Log
  1. Start your own log. Press Start my own log to clear the example data.
  2. Write your SMART goal. Give it a name, write it in one sentence and fill in each letter. Add milestones, the checkpoints that show you're on track.
  3. Add 2–4 daily habits that move your goal forward, and set how many days a week you aim for.
  4. Tick each day in the habit grid. Your week score at the top updates instantly.
  5. Every Monday, list your top priorities for the week.
  6. Every Sunday, rate your week and answer the review questions. Then press Weekly report (PDF) to download a summary you can keep or share.
  7. Everything saves automatically in this browser, and the Save button saves on demand. Use Settings → Download backup now and then so you never lose your log.
