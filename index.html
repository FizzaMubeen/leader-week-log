How to use Leader Week Log
  1. Start your own log. Press Start my own log to clear the example data.
  2. Write your SMART goal. Give it a name, write it in one sentence, fill in each letter and set the dates. Add milestones, then press Save goal. Use Edit to change it later.
  3. Add 2–4 daily habits that move your goal forward, and set how many days a week you aim for.
  4. Tick each day in the habit grid. Your score at the top updates instantly.
  5. Every Monday, list your top priorities for the week.
  6. Every Sunday, rate your week and answer the review questions.
  7. Look at any dates you like. Switch the header to Custom dates and choose a start and end date, or a quick pick such as Last 30 days. The score, habit grid, insights and PDF report all follow your dates.
  8. Download a PDF report for a week or your custom dates from the top bar.
  9. Everything saves automatically in this browser, and the Save button saves on demand. Use Settings → Download backup now and then so you never lose your log.
